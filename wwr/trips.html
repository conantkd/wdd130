Family Splash

Perfect for first-timers and families with children. This gentle 2-hour ride takes you through the beautiful, scenic lower sections of the Hidromiel river. Enjoy light rapids, opportunities to swim, and breathtaking wildlife sightings. No previous experience is required, and our expert guides ensure a safe, fun environment for everyone.

Hidromiel Rush

Our most popular trip! The Hidromiel Rush offers a fantastic blend of exciting Class III white-water action and stunning canyon views. Over the course of 4 hours, you will paddle through thrilling drops and waves. It is perfect for adventurous beginners and those who have rafted before. A riverside snack is included halfway through the journey.

The Extreme Canyon

For the ultimate adrenaline seekers, this full-day expedition tackles the upper canyon's roaring Class IV and V rapids. You will need teamwork, stamina, and a thirst for adventure to conquer the steep drops and fast currents. Previous rafting experience is highly recommended. Includes a hearty lunch and professional action photography.
