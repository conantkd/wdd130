Experience the ultimate adrenaline on the rivers of Antioquia with us. Our rafting tours offer the perfect blend of excitement and safety. Book your unforgettable nature escape today!

History

Founded in 1990, Rapidos Antioquia began as a small adventure among local friends exploring the thrilling waters of Hidromiel.

Over the decades, we became pioneers in local adventure tourism. Today, rafting with our expert guides means experiencing over 30 years of river tradition
